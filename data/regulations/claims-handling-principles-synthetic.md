# Synthetic Claims Handling Principles

This is an original synthetic paraphrase of themes associated with fair insurance claims handling. It is not copied regulatory wording, is not legal advice, and must not be represented as an authoritative statement of current rules.

## 1. Prompt and fair treatment

An insurer should assess a claim promptly and fairly, obtain information that is reasonably relevant, and avoid unnecessary delay. Where a decision depends on uncertain facts, the uncertainty should be investigated proportionately.

## 2. Customer guidance

Give the customer reasonable guidance about the information needed, the progress of the claim, and material reasons for delay. Communications should be clear enough for the customer to understand what is happening and what is expected next.

## 3. Reasoned outcomes

Do not reject a claim unreasonably. A decision should be consistent with the policy wording, endorsements, and relevant evidence. Explain the main facts and terms relied upon, and identify any significant information that remains unavailable.

## 4. Outcome focus

Consider whether the handling process is producing an appropriate outcome for the customer, not only whether a procedural step was completed. Escalate cases where a vulnerable customer, serious financial impact, or conflicting evidence calls for additional care.
