# Endorsement: Home Water Damage Clarification

This synthetic endorsement applies only when it is listed on the HOME-002 schedule. It clarifies the boundary between a sudden escape of water and water damage that develops over time.

## 1. Sudden escape remains covered

The cover in clause 3 continues to apply to sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed installation or domestic appliance. We may also pay reasonable tracing costs when locating the source is necessary to limit insured damage.

## 2. Gradual seepage remains excluded

For clause 4.3, gradual seepage includes repeated small leaks, water passing through failed seals over an extended period, and damage made worse by an unresolved maintenance issue. The date the damage is discovered is not, on its own, evidence of when it began.

## 3. Evidence and assessment

We will consider the likely duration and source of the water using available inspection findings, repair records, photographs, and other relevant evidence. This clause does not require rejection where the cause or duration is uncertain; the claim must be assessed on the evidence available.
