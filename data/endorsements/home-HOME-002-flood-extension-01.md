# Endorsement: Home Flood Extension

This synthetic endorsement applies only when it is listed on the HOME-002 schedule. It forms part of the policy from its effective date and takes precedence over inconsistent base wording for the clauses stated below.

## 1. Replacement of clause 4.2

Clause 4.2 is replaced. We cover direct physical loss to insured buildings and contents caused by flood, including surface water run-off that first accumulates outside the home and then enters it. The policy excess and the flood limit shown on the schedule apply.

This extension does not cover loss caused by gradual seepage, repeated leakage, condensation, or damp. Those causes remain subject to clause 4.3, even when a separate flood event occurs at another time.

## 2. Ground-floor contents

Ground-floor contents are covered for direct flood damage when the schedule includes contents cover. Items stored in a cellar or below ground level remain excluded unless the schedule specifically identifies them and states a separate limit.

## 3. No change to other terms

All policy terms not changed by this endorsement continue to apply. This endorsement does not cover the cost of repairing the source of a leak that is not itself insured.
