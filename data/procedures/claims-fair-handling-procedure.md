# Claims Fair Handling and Escalation Procedure

This synthetic internal procedure describes minimum handling steps for personal-lines and small-commercial property claims. It is a paraphrased training document, not regulatory text and not a substitute for current compliance guidance.

## 1. Acknowledge and guide

Confirm receipt of a claim, explain the next expected steps, and give the customer a clear route to provide relevant information. Tell the customer when an investigation is likely to take longer and why.

## 2. Make an evidence-led assessment

Identify the policy version and endorsements that applied on the loss date. Consider evidence both for and against the proposed outcome. Do not treat a missing document or an unresolved technical question as proof that an exclusion applies.

## 3. Decisions and escalation

- Escalate a proposed rejection based on an exclusion when the facts or wording are materially uncertain.
- Ask a technical lead to review conflicting reports or evidence of more than one cause.
- Record the relevant clauses, evidence, customer communications, and reasons for the outcome.
- Explain any decision in plain language, including important evidence that was not available.

## 4. Service and complaints

Handle claims without avoidable delay and treat customers consistently while taking individual circumstances into account. If a customer expresses dissatisfaction, follow the complaint-handling process and explain how to raise a formal complaint.
