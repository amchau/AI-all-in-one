# Northstar Small Business Property Policy

This synthetic wording applies to the business premises and property listed on the schedule. The business must use the premises for the declared trade and must tell us about a material change in occupancy or activity.

## 1. Insured property

We cover the buildings, business equipment, and stock listed on the schedule against insured physical loss or damage occurring during the period of insurance. Limits and excesses are stated separately for each category.

## 2. Accidental damage

Where accidental damage cover is selected, we cover sudden, unforeseen physical damage to insured property unless a specific exclusion applies. Damage caused by a deliberate act of the insured is not accidental.

## 3. Escape of water

We cover sudden and accidental damage to insured property caused by water escaping from a fixed water or heating installation at the premises. We do not cover the cost of repairing the part from which water escaped unless an endorsement expressly adds that cost.

## 4. Flood

Flood means water escaping from the sea, a river, a canal, a reservoir, or another watercourse and temporarily covering normally dry land. Flood cover applies only if the schedule shows the flood extension. If it is not shown, direct flood damage is excluded.

## 5. Business records and mitigation

Keep reasonable records of stock and equipment values. After damage, take practical steps to protect unaffected property and keep invoices for emergency work. We will consider reasonable mitigation costs when they relate to an insured event.
