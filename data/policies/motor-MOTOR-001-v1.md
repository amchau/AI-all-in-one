# Northstar Motor Policy

This synthetic wording covers the vehicle identified on the schedule during the period of insurance. Cover depends on the selected level shown on the schedule and any active endorsement.

## 1. Comprehensive accidental damage

If comprehensive cover is shown on the schedule, we cover sudden and accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle. The standard excess applies to each incident. We may repair the vehicle, replace it, or make a cash settlement, taking account of its market value immediately before the loss.

## 2. Fire and theft

We cover loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by fire, attempted theft, or theft, subject to the schedule and the security conditions provided with the vehicle.

## 3. Flood and weather events

Flood means water temporarily covering normally dry ground after escaping from a river, canal, reservoir, or the sea. Comprehensive cover includes direct physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by flood. It does not include mechanical failure caused only by attempting to start a vehicle after water has entered its engine.

## 4. What is not covered

We do not cover gradual deterioration, mechanical or electrical failure that is not caused by an insured event, or damage arising while the vehicle is used outside the permitted use on the schedule.

## 5. Claims duties

Report an incident promptly, provide the information reasonably requested, and do not authorise permanent repairs before we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect the vehicle. Emergency work needed to prevent immediate further damage may be carried out and evidenced with receipts and photographs.
